## 4. Information needed to use Telegram through FenixUz

FenixUz is a client for the Telegram messaging network. To deliver
messages, calls, and other Telegram features, FenixUz exchanges data
**directly with Telegram's servers** using the open Telegram MTProto
protocol. This includes, for example:

- Your phone number, Telegram account identifier, profile name, photo,
  username, and any biographical information you choose to provide.
- The contents of messages, media, files, stickers, reactions, calls,
  and stories you send or receive.
- Contacts you choose to sync with Telegram, if you enable that feature
  in settings.
- Connection metadata required for the protocol (IP address, approximate
  region, device platform, time of connection).

**This information is processed by Telegram, not by Vipads LLC.**
We have no servers of our own that receive or store this data. How
Telegram uses that information is described in Telegram's
[Privacy Policy](https://telegram.org/privacy) and
[Terms of Service](https://telegram.org/tos).

If you sign out of Telegram or delete your Telegram account, the
relevant data on Telegram's servers is removed according to Telegram's
policy. We cannot delete or modify data stored on Telegram's
infrastructure on your behalf.

## 5. Data stored locally on your device

FenixUz stores the following information **only on your computer**, in
the user's application data folder (typically
`%APPDATA%\FenixUz` on Windows):

- **Session data and authorization keys** required to keep you signed
  in to Telegram between launches.
- **Cached chats, media, stickers, and themes** so the application can
  show content quickly and work offline.
- **Your in-app preferences**, including FenixUz-specific feature
  toggles such as Ghost Mode and Save Deleted Messages, stored in
  `tdata/fenix_settings.json`.
- **A local-only list of messages that other users deleted in your
  chats**, when the "Save Deleted Messages" feature is enabled by you.
  This list is stored in `tdata/fenix_deleted.json`. It is never
  uploaded anywhere; only you can read it, and it is removed when you
  uninstall the app or sign out.
- **Crash minidumps**, if a fatal error occurs, written to the
  application's `tdata` folder so that you can choose to share them
  with us manually. They are not transmitted automatically.

You can remove all of this local data at any time by signing out from
inside the app or by uninstalling FenixUz through Windows Settings.

## 6. FenixUz-specific features and your privacy

FenixUz adds optional privacy and convenience features on top of the
standard Telegram client. None of them transmit information to us or to
any third party other than Telegram, and several of them deliberately
**reduce** the information shared with Telegram:

- **Ghost Mode.** When enabled, FenixUz refuses to broadcast your
  online presence, typing/recording indicators, and read receipts to
  Telegram's servers while you are using the app. This means *less*
  information about you is sent to Telegram while Ghost Mode is on.
- **Save Deleted Messages.** When enabled, messages that other users
  delete in your chats are kept in your local view with a small
  "deleted by sender / by channel" label. The label and the underlying
  list are stored only on your device. Messages you delete yourself
  are still removed normally.
- **FenixUz Features section in Settings.** A unified place to
  configure these toggles. Settings are stored locally in JSON files
  inside `tdata`.

## 9. Security

To protect your information, FenixUz:

- Uses the encrypted Telegram MTProto transport for all messaging
  traffic.
- Stores local session data and cached content in the user's protected
  Windows application data folder, so it is accessible only to the
  signed-in Windows user.
- Supports the standard Telegram local passcode and two-factor
  authentication features.
- Is distributed exclusively through the Microsoft Store and our
  authorized download channels, signed and verified by Microsoft.

No system can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your
FenixUz installation has been compromised, please sign out of all
Telegram sessions from the app and contact us at
support@vipads.uz.

## 12. Your rights

Because FenixUz does not collect personal information about you on our
servers, there is no profile we can access, modify, or delete on your
behalf. You retain full control over your data:

- **Access / portability.** You can export your Telegram data at any
  time using the in-app export feature
  (Settings → Advanced → Export Telegram data).
- **Correction.** You can edit your profile information directly inside
  Telegram.
- **Erasure.** You can delete individual messages, leave chats, sign
  out of FenixUz, or delete your Telegram account. Uninstalling
  FenixUz removes all data the app stored locally on your device.
- **Restriction / objection.** You can disable any feature in
  Settings, including FenixUz-specific features, at any time.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom,
or another jurisdiction that gives you statutory privacy rights
(GDPR, UK GDPR, the Personal Data Law of Uzbekistan, etc.), you may
exercise those rights by contacting us at support@vipads.uz. We will
respond within the time required by the applicable law.
